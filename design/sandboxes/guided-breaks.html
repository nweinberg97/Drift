Guided breaks

Breaks that tell you what to do: move, walk, hydrate, snack or breathe. Temro picks one for you each time and rotates, so there's nothing to decide. Everything below is clickable; time runs fast so you can watch a whole round.

1 · During a Pomodoro

Time's up, here's your break

When a focus round ends, the widget turns into a break card: the type, what to do, and a countdown.

  • Swap picks a different activity of the same type.
  • Done ends the break early and goes back to work; Skip goes straight on.
  • Movements come with two or three short steps. Everything else is one line.
  • The line under the timer tells you which break is coming next.
2 · In a session

Type breaks right into your plan

Add a break type and a length between your blocks. Temro recognises move, walk, water or hydrate, snack and breathe, plus plain break for "pick for me".

The same works in the planner: any time block can be switched to a break.

Breaks: move walk water snack breathe break
3 · Your Pomodoro break plan

Choose which breaks come up

Short breaks rotate through the types you pick. The long break after round four gets its own type: a walk works well.

  • Click a type below the cycle to include or leave it out.
  • Rotate goes in order; Mix shuffles without repeating the last one.
4 · Settings › Breaks

The break library

Five types, each with a starter set. Switch off the ones you don't like, or add your own; Temro only picks from what's switched on.

Try it: open Snack and add "Handful of almonds".
