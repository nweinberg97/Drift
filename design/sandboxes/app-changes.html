Drift: what's changing

Try each change here before I build it into the app. Everything below is clickable. The landing page has its own preview.

1 · Time blocks

Split into as many blocks as you like

Today you can only split evenly into 2, 3 or 4. Now a + after the 4 keeps going, and turns into a − / + counter once you're past 4.

  • Up to 12 blocks, never shorter than 5 minutes each.
  • The bar and the list update as you click.
  • Renaming and resizing single blocks works as before.
Try it: pick 2 hours, then press + until you hit 8.

2 · Time's up

Still catches your eye, without jumping around

Today the whole widget jumps sideways and tilts, so Done moves out from under your pointer. Now the widget stays put:

  • A small nudge inside the card, three points at most, every four seconds.
  • The bell rings and the border glows, so it still stands out with the sound off.
  • Everything stops the moment your pointer reaches the card, so Done is always still when you click it.
  • Done is a wide button; Return works too.
Press "Finish the timer".
3 · Plan a work session

Drag the session like a physical block

The session now follows your pointer exactly while you drag, then settles onto the nearest step when you let go.

  • Grab anywhere on the session to move it; the ends show handles on hover to resize.
  • A label above it shows the new time as you drag, and a dashed outline shows where it was.
  • Drag toward either side and the timeline scrolls by itself, so you can move a session across the whole day.
  • Drag on empty space to scroll the day. Arrow keys nudge by one step.
Try it: drag the session past the right edge to move it into the evening.

4 · Name it

Say the task instead of typing it

Click Name it and a small mic appears on the right of the box. Type as usual, or click the mic and say it. It stops listening when you pause, and the name is saved.

  • "Name it" now sits on the same line as the timer's numbers.
  • Speech is recognised on your Mac, as in the launcher.
  • Esc cancels; Return saves what's there.
Try it: click Name it, then the mic.
5 · Behind the scenes

The widget comes back after sleep and restarts

Nothing to try here: it's a fix. After you open the lid, log in, or plug in or remove a display, Drift will check the widget is on a screen you can see and bring it back if it isn't (for example when it was last on a display that's no longer connected). It also re-shows itself if macOS hid it while the Mac slept.
