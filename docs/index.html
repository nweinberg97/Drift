Name it, time it, get back to work.

Press ⌥ Space in any app and type what you're doing and for how long, or when. A small timer stays in the corner of your screen while you work. Click it to rename, change the time, add another or see today's plan.

Nothing to sign up for.

Drift is a timer, not a productivity platform.

AccountsStreaksDashboardsSubscriptionsAnalyticsNagging

Offline, always

The app has no network access at all. Timers and settings stay on your Mac.

Voice stays local

Speech is recognised on-device, only while you're talking, and never stored.

Out of your way

No Dock icon and no windows to manage. It lives in the menu bar and one small corner of your screen.
