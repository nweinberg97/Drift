Nothing to sign up for.

Drift is a timer, not a productivity platform.

AccountsStreaksDashboardsSubscriptionsAnalyticsNagging

Offline, always

The app has no network access at all. Timers, sessions and settings stay on your Mac.

Voice stays local

Speech is recognised on-device, only while you're talking, and never stored.

Only what you use

No Dock icon and no windows to manage. Don't plan your day or use Pomodoro, focus sounds or voice? Switch them off in Settings and they're gone from every menu. Nothing is deleted.
