Privacy

Short version: Drift collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics and no network access, so nothing you do in it can leave your Mac.

Data collected: none. Not by us, not by anyone we work with. There's no "us" on the other end: the app never connects to the internet.

What stays on your Mac

To work, Drift keeps a few things in its own private folder on your Mac:

  • Your timers, so they survive a restart.
  • Your scheduled blocks and the days you skipped or changed them.
  • Your settings, shortcuts and a short list of recent task names.

macOS keeps this folder separate from other apps (the App Sandbox). Nothing in it is uploaded or synced. Deleting the app and the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.nweinberg.drift removes all of it.

Microphone and speech

Voice is optional. Drift asks for the microphone and speech recognition only the first time you choose to speak a timer. Then:

  • It listens only after you press the shortcut or the mic button, and stops when you stop talking.
  • Speech is turned into text on your Mac using Apple's on-device recognition. If on-device recognition isn't available, Drift doesn't fall back to a server; it simply asks you to type.
  • Audio and transcripts are never saved.

Notifications

Drift schedules its "timer done" and "block starts now" notifications with macOS on your Mac. They contain your timer's name and are shown only to you.

No tracking, no third parties

Drift contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party services. It does not use your location, contacts, files, camera or browsing.

Purchases

If you bought Drift on the Mac App Store, Apple handles the payment. We don't receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that part: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Drift collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date below, before any version of the app does anything differently.
