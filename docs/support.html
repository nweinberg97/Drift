Getting started

Drift lives in the menu bar and a small widget in the top-left corner of your screen. There's no Dock icon. To reopen the welcome guide, click the menu bar icon → Help → Getting Started.

  1. Press ⌥ Space from any app.
  2. Write what you're working on and for how long: Reply to emails 25m. Press Return.
  3. The timer starts in the widget. Click its name to rename it, its time to change it.
  4. With nothing running, the widget offers ▶ Start 25m and one-tap lengths, so you can also start from there.

Sessions, later today and routines

List a few time blocks with commas, then, or one per line, and they run back to back as one session. Between blocks a card says what's next and starts it after 10 seconds (change this in Settings → Next time block starts). Time blocks don't get extra time unless you turn on Extra time in sessions.

Under what you type, Start now · Later today · Routine shows what Return will do. Drift picks it from your words (“at 2pm” is later today, “weekdays at 9” is a routine); tap one and the text changes to match, then pick a time in the At row (and days in the On row for a routine). A card at the top of the widget confirms it: PLANNED or ROUTINE ADDED, with Got it, Edit and Undo. You can also use the menu bar → More Ways to Start → Plan Later Today… or New Routine…

When the time comes, Drift chimes and shows a card: Start, In 5 min or Skip today. If you ignore it, it starts by itself after a minute, and it still ends on time.

Click a planned item in the widget or menu bar to change it: drag it on the timeline (5-minute steps; hold ⌥ for single minutes), click the start or end time to type it, nudge it by −5, −1, +1 or +5 minutes, or pick the days it repeats. For a routine, choose Just today or every day it repeats.

Common questions

The widget disappeared

Press ⌥ T, or click the menu bar icon → Show Timer Widget. If it's off-screen after changing displays: Settings → Appearance → Move to Top Left.

⌥ Space doesn't open the launcher

Another app (Raycast, Alfred, an input-source shortcut) may already use it. Open Settings and pick another combination for New timer, or change the other app's shortcut.

Voice doesn't work

Allow Drift in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition. Drift only uses on-device recognition; if it says that isn't available, turn on Dictation once in System Settings → Keyboard so macOS downloads your language.

I don't get notifications

System Settings → Notifications → Drift → Allow notifications. Also check Settings → Sound → Notification when a timer finishes. The chime plays even when notifications are off.

The chime keeps going, or the widget shakes

By design, when time's up the chime repeats every few seconds (for up to two minutes) and the widget shakes until you touch it. Click the widget, then Done, +5m or Restart. Prefer one chime? Settings → Sound → When a timer finishes → Chime once. To stop the shake, turn off Settings → Shake the widget when time's up.

+5m is greyed out

In a session, time blocks don't stretch unless Settings → Extra time in sessions is on. If you've set a Grace period, +5m stops once a timer or time block has used it up.

How do I delete something I planned?

Hover its row in the widget's Today list and click ×, or right-click it → Delete (Delete Routine for one that repeats). In the menu bar, each Today row has Edit…, Skip Today and Delete; the editor has Delete too. Changed your mind? Click Undo in the bar at the top of the widget within a few seconds. To miss it just once, use Skip Today instead.

Pomodoro, voice or planning is missing

Check Settings → Features. Plan my day, Focus sounds, Pomodoro and Voice can each be switched off, which hides them everywhere. Turning one back on brings everything back; nothing is deleted.

Start Drift when I log in

Settings → Open Drift at login.

Does it work offline? Does it sync?

It only works offline: Drift has no network access at all. Nothing syncs between Macs.

Uninstall

Quit from the menu bar icon, then drag Drift from Applications to the Trash. To remove its saved timers and settings too, delete ~/Library/Containers/com.nweinberg.drift.
